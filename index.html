FROM THE STREET TO THE FIRST TEAM
The Problem

Talent doesn't disappear. It just never gets a pitch to stand on.

Townships and under-resourced communities across South Africa lack safe, maintained sports facilities. Without a pitch or a structured programme, young people — including those already exposed to drugs, unemployment, or life on the streets — have nowhere to turn ability into opportunity.

Our Programme

Three phases. One growing pitch count.

Each phase builds on proven ground before expanding further.

01
YEAR 1 — PILOT

FIRST PITCH

Build the first township pitch, supply kits, recruit coaches and mentors, launch the first talent pathway cohort.

02
YEAR 2 — PROVINCIAL

WIDENING REACH

Expand township-by-township per province, formalising partnerships with SAFA regional structures and local clubs.

03
YEAR 3+ — NATIONAL

FULL SQUAD

Scale nationally with an established M&E framework, coaching pipeline, and pitch maintenance plan.

What We Deliver

Four things a talented kid rarely gets all at once.

Each pillar reinforces the others — a pitch without coaching goes quiet, talent without a pathway goes nowhere.

01

Pitch & Facility

Levelled, fenced, and lit pitches built to last in the community that needs them.

02

Kits & Equipment

Full kits, boots, balls, and goalposts sized to each site and squad.

03

Coaching & Mentorship

Qualified coaches paired with life-skills and drug-awareness mentors.

04

Talent Pathway

Trials and scouting links to clubs and academies, with paid opportunities for standout players.
